\# CharitMe Product Principles



Every feature must satisfy these principles.



\## AI First



AI should proactively help users, not wait for instructions.



Examples:



Suggest better campaign titles.



Improve storytelling.



Predict fundraising success.



Recommend sharing times.



Detect fraud.



Identify duplicate campaigns.



Generate thank-you messages.



Recommend donors.



Generate tax summaries.



Create videos.



Create images.



Translate automatically.



\---



\## Trust



Every action increases trust.



Verified organizers.



Transparent payments.



Identity verification.



Fraud detection.



Public audit trail.



Impact tracking.



\---



\## Frictionless



Campaign in under five minutes.



Donate in under thirty seconds.



No unnecessary forms.



No unnecessary clicks.



No unnecessary complexity.



\---



\## Accessibility



WCAG AA minimum.



Keyboard support.



Screen readers.



High contrast.



Responsive.



\---



\## World Class



Every experience should be better than GoFundMe.



Never copy competitors.



Innovate.



Delight users.



Think globally.



Optimize relentlessly.
